Features

Everything in one place

From building your CV to finding and saving jobs — without juggling five different tools.

CV Builder

Fill in your experience, education, and skills. CVora formats everything into a clean one-page PDF — no design skills needed.

Job Matching

CVora reads your CV and searches live listings to surface roles that match your background — not just keyword guesses.

Upload Your CV

Already have one? Upload it as a PDF and CVora will read it, extract your profile, and start matching you to jobs straight away.

Saved Profiles

Keep multiple CVs — one for engineering roles, one for management, whatever you need. Edit and download them anytime.

Save Jobs

Bookmark listings you like so they're easy to find later. All your saved jobs live in one place on your dashboard.

Your Data, Your Account

CVs are stored securely and only visible to you. Nothing is shared or used for anything other than generating your results.

How it works

Three steps

No setup, no configuration. Just fill in your details and go.

1

Build or upload your CV

Use the step-by-step builder, or upload a PDF you already have. Either way takes a few minutes.

2

Tell us what you're looking for

Set your preferred role, location, and experience level. CVora uses this alongside your CV to find relevant listings.

3

Browse matches and apply

You get a ranked list of jobs that actually fit your background. Click through to the listing and apply directly.
