Frequently asked questions

Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. You can cancel your Pro subscription at any time from your profile. Your plan stays active until the end of the billing period.
Do my free limits reset each month?
Yes. Free plan limits (uploads, builds, searches) reset on the first of each calendar month.
What happens if I hit 50 uploads on Pro?
Your counter resets next month. If you need more within the same month, contact us and we will sort it out.
Is my payment information secure?
All payments are processed by Stripe. CVora never stores your card details.
What does "unlimited" mean for builds and searches?
No monthly cap. Our fair-use rate limits still apply (20 requests per hour) to keep the service fast for everyone.
