Refund Policy

Last updated: May 2026

We want you to be happy with CVora. If something isn't right, we'll make it right.

7-Day Money-Back Guarantee

If you subscribe to CVora Pro and are not satisfied, you may request a full refund within 7 days of your initial purchase. No questions asked.

How to Request a Refund

Email us at cvora.contact@gmail.com with the subject line "Refund Request" and include the email address associated with your account. We will process your refund within 3–5 business days.

Renewals

The 7-day guarantee applies to your first payment only. Renewal charges are not refundable, but you can cancel at any time from your Profile screen to prevent future charges. You retain Pro access until the end of your current billing period.

Exceptions

Refunds will not be issued if your account has been suspended for violating our Terms of Service.

To cancel your subscription, go to Profile → Subscription → Cancel subscription inside the app. For refunds, contact cvora.contact@gmail.com.
